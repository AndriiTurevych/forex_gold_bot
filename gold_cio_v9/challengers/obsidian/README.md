# Strategy Challenger #1 — ICT Obsidian v0.1

Status: CHALLENGER ONLY. Not eligible to emit production/live signals.

## Evidence discipline

There is not yet a sufficiently clear, formal, source-verifiable public specification of the ICT Obsidian Model to justify treating any inferred rule set as canonical. v0.1 therefore records a falsifiable reconstruction hypothesis only. Exact rules must be refined from primary-source examples/video frames and any rule change after observing outcomes requires a new version/experiment ID.

## Reconstructed v0.1 sequence

1. H4/H1 directional context.
2. Liquidity map: PDH/PDL, session highs/lows, equal highs/lows, external liquidity.
3. Displacement leg.
4. Retracement/consolidation after displacement.
5. Sweep of local liquidity.
6. MSS/CISD in the direction of the primary scenario.
7. FVG/OB/repricing zone as entry location.
8. Invalidation beyond the swept extreme.
9. Target = opposing liquidity.
10. TAKE candidate only when projected R:R >= 2.0 and the independent deterministic Risk/VETO layer does not reject it.

Otherwise it remains a challenger.
